# Expanding Horizon Journal Production Launch Checklist

## Institutional
* Confirm legal ownership and publisher name.
* Confirm Editor in Chief, Managing Editor and editorial roles.
* Constitute an international editorial board.
* Establish governance and conflict of interest procedures.

## Publishing
* Apply for or confirm ISSN.
* Select DOI registration route.
* Decide final open access licence.
* Decide article processing charge policy, if any.
* Establish plagiarism checking and manuscript review procedures.
* Establish article production and archiving workflow.

## Website
* Replace placeholders.
* Connect the submission system.
* Add verified ISSN only after issuance.
* Add DOI links only after registration.
* Add indexing services only after acceptance.
* Add real issues, articles and metadata.
* Publish final legal and privacy notices.
