FOR AUTHORS

Guidance for submitting scholarly work.

Expanding Horizon Journal welcomes rigorous original scholarship across the social sciences, humanities, management, education and related interdisciplinary fields.

Article types

Research Articles

Original empirical or theoretical studies with a clear research question and substantive contribution.

Review Articles

Critical, systematic or integrative reviews that advance understanding.

Conceptual Articles

Theoretical or conceptual contributions that develop or connect existing ideas.

Short Research Communications

Concise presentations of significant findings or interventions.

Book Reviews

Critical assessments of scholarly books.

Before submission

  • The manuscript must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.
  • Follow the journal's formatting and referencing requirements.
  • Identify all substantive contributors as authors.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest and relevant funding.
  • Comply with applicable ethical requirements.
  • Authors remain responsible for accuracy and integrity.
