AUTHOR GUIDELINES

Preparing a manuscript for submission.

Guidance for authors preparing original scholarly work for Expanding Horizon Journal.

General requirements

  • Submit original work not under consideration elsewhere.
  • Use clear academic language and an appropriate scholarly structure.
  • Provide a title, author information, abstract and keywords.
  • Use a consistent scholarly referencing system appropriate to the discipline.
  • Identify funding and conflicts of interest where applicable.
  • Include ethical approval or participant consent statements where relevant.
  • Ensure all substantive contributors are appropriately recognised as authors.

Double blind review preparation

Where double blind review applies, remove author names, affiliations, acknowledgements and other identifying information from the review manuscript. A separate title page should contain the author details.

Self citations should be presented in a way that preserves anonymity without obscuring the scholarly record.

Recommended structure

Research articles should normally contain an introduction, engagement with relevant scholarship, methodology or research design, analysis or findings, discussion and conclusion, adapted to the discipline and article type.

Review and conceptual articles may use structures appropriate to their purpose while maintaining a clear argument, evidence base and scholarly contribution.

Use concise headings, accurate references, tables and figures where they genuinely support the argument.

READY TO SUBMIT?

Send the manuscript when it is ready.

Use the online submission form to provide the manuscript, title page, abstract, keywords and required declarations.
