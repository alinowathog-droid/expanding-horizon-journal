PUBLISHING & VISIBILITY

More than publication. Built for discoverability.

APCUS$30Article Processing Charge

We are building the journal around persistent researcher identities, DOI based publication metadata and broad scholarly discovery. Each service has a distinct role in helping authors establish, find and connect their work.

iD ORCID

Researcher identity

Authors can connect their work to a persistent ORCID iD, helping distinguish their scholarship from researchers with similar names and improving attribution across research systems.

AUTHOR IDENTITY
Crossref

DOI & metadata

Crossref registration gives published articles persistent DOI links and structured metadata that can be reused by scholarly discovery and citation systems.

GLOBAL SCHOLARLY INFRASTRUCTURE
⌕ Google Scholar

Article discovery

Articles will be prepared to meet Google Scholar's technical requirements so researchers can discover published work through one of the world's major scholarly search services.

SCHOLARLY SEARCH
DOAJ

Open access discovery

We intend to pursue DOAJ inclusion when the journal meets its eligibility requirements. Inclusion would extend article metadata into a wide network of open access discovery services and library systems.
