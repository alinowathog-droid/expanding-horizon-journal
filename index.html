ABOUT THE JOURNAL

A scholarly space for expanding horizons.

Expanding Horizon Journal is an independent international multidisciplinary academic journal devoted to the publication of rigorous and original scholarship across the social sciences, humanities, management, education and related fields.

The journal welcomes empirical, theoretical, conceptual and interdisciplinary contributions that engage significant questions and make a clear contribution to knowledge.

AIMS & SCOPE

Across disciplines, with scholarly purpose.

Social Sciences

Governance, political science, public administration, international relations, sociology, economics, development, peace and conflict, and public policy.

Humanities

History, philosophy, religious studies, literature, languages, cultural studies and related fields.

Management & Business

Management, leadership, organisational studies, entrepreneurship, human resources and public sector management.

Education

Higher education, educational policy, administration, curriculum, pedagogy and educational development.

Development & Public Affairs

Development policy, public affairs, environmental governance, political ecology and sustainability.

Interdisciplinary Research

Research that crosses conventional disciplinary boundaries to address complex social, institutional and human questions.
