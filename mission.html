JOURNAL IDENTITY

Mission, aims and scholarly purpose.

Expanding Horizon Journal is an independent international multidisciplinary academic journal committed to rigorous scholarship and constructive intellectual exchange across disciplinary boundaries.

MISSION

To expand the horizons of knowledge.

The journal provides an independent scholarly platform for original and intellectually significant work across the social sciences, humanities, management, education and related interdisciplinary fields.

It encourages research that engages important questions, communicates evidence clearly and contributes meaningfully to scholarly debate.

AIMS

What the journal seeks to do.

  • Publish rigorous original scholarship.
  • Encourage dialogue across disciplinary boundaries.
  • Provide space for empirical, theoretical and conceptual research.
  • Support emerging and established scholars through transparent editorial processes.
  • Promote responsible, ethical and accessible scholarly communication.
