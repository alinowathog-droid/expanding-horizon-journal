POLICIES

Transparent standards for responsible publishing.

These policy foundations will govern the journal before the first issue opens for submissions.

Peer Review →Publication Ethics →AI and Authorship →Research Integrity →Corrections & Retractions →Conflicts of Interest →

PEER REVIEW

Double blind review.

Suitable manuscripts undergo editorial screening and external peer review. Reviewer identities are concealed from authors in the review process. Editorial decisions remain the responsibility of the journal.

PUBLICATION ETHICS

Integrity throughout publishing.

The journal addresses plagiarism, duplicate publication, fabrication, falsification, authorship disputes, conflicts of interest, confidentiality, complaints, appeals and editorial misconduct.

AI AND AUTHORSHIP

Human accountability remains central.

Appropriate AI tools may assist with language, translation or research tasks where permitted, but AI systems cannot be authors. Authors remain responsible for accuracy, originality, citations, data and integrity.

Research integrity

The journal expects honest reporting, accurate attribution, responsible data practices and disclosure of relevant ethical issues.

Corrections and retractions

Material errors or integrity concerns will be assessed and corrected or retracted where necessary, with transparent notices.

Conflicts of interest

Authors, reviewers and editors should disclose relationships or interests that could reasonably affect scholarly judgment.
