PRIVACY

Responsible handling of author and reviewer information.

This preliminary policy describes the journal's intended approach and should be legally reviewed before the production submission system is activated.

Information collected

The journal may collect information necessary for manuscript submission, editorial communication, peer review and publication, including names, professional affiliations, email addresses and scholarly identifiers.

Confidentiality

Information relating to unpublished manuscripts and confidential peer review should be handled securely and disclosed only where necessary for legitimate editorial or publishing purposes.
