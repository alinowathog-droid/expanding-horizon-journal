FOR REVIEWERS

Reviewing with fairness, rigor and confidentiality.

Reviewers contribute to the quality of the scholarly record through objective and constructive assessment.

Review principles

  • Assess scholarly merit rather than personal preference.
  • Maintain confidentiality and do not share manuscript material.
  • Declare conflicts of interest before accepting a review.
  • Provide specific, evidence based and respectful comments.
  • Do not use unpublished manuscript material for personal advantage.
  • Alert the editor to serious ethical or integrity concerns.
  • Return reviews within the agreed timeframe or request an extension.

What a useful review covers

Consider the manuscript's originality, relevance, research question, engagement with scholarship, methodology or reasoning, evidence, analysis, interpretation, structure, clarity and contribution.

Recommendations should be supported by comments that help the editor and authors understand the basis of the assessment.

Editorial decision

Reviewer recommendations inform but do not determine the final editorial decision. Additional specialist advice may be sought where necessary.
