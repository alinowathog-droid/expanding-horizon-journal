MANUSCRIPT SUBMISSION

Submit your scholarship to Expanding Horizon Journal.

The journal is preparing its launch submission workflow. Authors can review the requirements now and contact the editorial office when the manuscript system opens.

SUBMISSION READINESS

Prepare before the system opens.

Before submission, authors should ensure that the manuscript is original, clearly presented, ethically conducted where applicable, and not simultaneously under consideration by another journal.

  • Prepare the complete manuscript and references.
  • Remove identifying information from the manuscript for double blind review.
  • Prepare a separate title page with author information.
  • Include funding, conflict of interest and ethics declarations where applicable.
  • Confirm that all listed authors meet appropriate authorship standards.
  • Check the Author Guidelines before submission.

PEER REVIEW

Independent editorial assessment.

Submissions will undergo editorial screening before suitable manuscripts are sent for double blind peer review. Editorial decisions will be based on scholarly quality, originality, relevance to the journal and the standards described in the journal policies.

The journal does not guarantee acceptance or publication of any submission.

LAUNCH CYCLE

Submission system coming next.

The manuscript submission workflow will be activated for the launch cycle. Until then, authors may contact the editorial office with questions.
