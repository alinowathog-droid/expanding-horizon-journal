MANUSCRIPT SUBMISSION

Submit your scholarship to Expanding Horizon Journal.

The journal accepts original research, review, conceptual and other scholarly contributions within its multidisciplinary scope. Please read the Author Guidelines before submitting.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Prepare the manuscript.

Prepare an anonymised manuscript for double blind review and a separate title page containing author details. Have your abstract, keywords, declarations and manuscript files ready before completing the form.

  • The work is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.
  • All authors have approved the submission and meet appropriate authorship standards.
  • Identifying information has been removed from the review manuscript.
  • Funding, conflicts of interest and ethics information are disclosed where applicable.
  • The manuscript follows the journal's Author Guidelines.

ONLINE SUBMISSION

Manuscript submission form.

Complete the form carefully. Required fields are marked. The editorial office will receive the submission for editorial screening.

AFTER SUBMISSION

What happens next?

1. Receipt. The submission enters the journal's editorial record.

2. Editorial screening. The editor checks scope, completeness, originality concerns and basic scholarly suitability.

3. Peer review. Suitable manuscripts may be sent for double blind peer review.

4. Decision. The editor communicates the decision and any required revisions.
